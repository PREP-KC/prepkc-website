# **College & Career Specialist (Full-Time)**

Reports to: Director of College & Career Experiences 

Collaborates with: College & Career Team and across PREP-KC teams (as needed). 

## **Position Overview**

The College & Career Specialist provides strategic oversight of college and career opportunities to support the development of a K-12 continuum of experiences and to lead community mapping efforts to build a network of supporters interested in engaging with their district/schools, ensuring consistent quality across school partnerships. This individual will work closely with schools, volunteers, families, and community partners to deliver high-quality college- and career-readiness experiences for students. The Specialist serves as a connector among PREP-KC staff, educators, and business and community partners to ensure smooth coordination and impactful engagement opportunities

##  **Key Responsibilities**

### **Leadership and Collaboration**

* Support community mapping efforts with districts to build district volunteer networks by engaging alumni, families, and community partners.  
* Co-facilitate discussions with building administration, central office, and key building staff to build a K-12 continuum of college & career experiences together with district partners.  
* Support consistency of best practices across school partnerships and student experiences  
* Contribute to team meetings and cross-team projects to strengthen PREP-KC’s collective impact.

**Family and Community Engagement**

* Build awareness and relationships with alums, families, and community organizations to expand understanding of PREP-KC’s college and career experiences.  
* Lead key discussions with school partners to identify and build on their existing networks.  
* Attend alumni and family engagement events (e.g., career nights, parent information sessions, community showcases)  
* Communicate with alums and families about PREP-KC volunteer opportunities  
* Gather feedback from alums, families, and community partners to inform continuous improvement of K–8 programming and volunteer experiences  
* Support the development of marketing materials to share with families and community organizations in tandem with school partners 

### **Volunteer Engagement**

* Collaborate with the College and Career Liaisons to support strategic outreach to interested volunteers and partners for in-person and virtual experiences.  
* Maintain strong relationships with volunteers and partners, and support recognition and retention efforts across PREP-KC teams.

### 

### **Data and Impact Tracking**

* Support data collection and tracking for student participation and volunteer engagement  
* Provide district and school staff with training and coaching on using database tools to support volunteer engagement in their own communities  
* Collaborate with staff to ensure accurate, timely reporting of event data

Other duties as assigned.

## **Qualifications**

**Education & Experience**

* Bachelor’s degree in education, communications, social work, nonprofit management, or a related field (or equivalent work experience)  
* Experience in event coordination, volunteer management, or educational programming is preferred.  
* Experience in designing and implementing community and family engagement, community organizing, or similar programming  
* Familiarity with K–12 education systems and/or college- and career-readiness programs in urban school districts is a plus.  
* Experience facilitating and coaching adult discussions to build a shared vision is preferred.

**Skills & Competencies**

* Strong organizational skills and attention to detail  
* Excellent verbal and written communication skills  
* Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment  
* Strong interpersonal skills and a commitment to relationship-building  
* Comfortable working both independently and collaboratively within a team  
* Proficiency with data entry, queries, and reporting within a database/CRM platform  
* Proficient in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace
